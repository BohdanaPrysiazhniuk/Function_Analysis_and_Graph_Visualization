**Project Overview**

This repository presents the visual design and layout of an educational presentation explaining the process of function analysis and graph construction.  
The presentation applies principles of visual communication to guide users through each stage of mathematical analysis. The content is organized into sequential sections that illustrate the workflow - from identifying function properties to analyzing derivatives, asymptotes, and the final graph - using a clean, consistent, and easy-to-follow design.

**Design Specifications & Features**

_Style & Aesthetics_

Academic Minimalist Design: The presentation adopts a clean, modern academic style that prioritizes clarity, logical progression, and readability. Every slide follows a structured composition that supports the sequential nature of mathematical problem-solving.  
Color Palette: A restrained color scheme with subtle accent colors highlights important formulas, graph elements, and analytical stages without distracting from the mathematical content.  
Typography: A clear sans-serif typeface establishes a strong visual hierarchy through section titles, mathematical notation, explanatory text, and conclusions, ensuring comfortable reading throughout the presentation.  
Grid & Layout System: Each slide follows a consistent grid system that aligns equations, diagrams, and annotations. This creates visual consistency and allows readers to focus on the analytical process rather than navigating changing layouts.  
Mathematical Visualization: Graphs, sign tables, derivative diagrams, and asymptote illustrations are integrated into the layout to visually support each analytical step, reducing reliance on text-heavy explanations.

_Tools & Workflow_

Canva: Used to design the presentation layout, organize mathematical content, and establish a consistent visual hierarchy across all slides.

**Design Assets**

_Educational Presentation_

A presentation illustrating the complete process of function analysis. The information is presented through structured layouts, mathematical diagrams, sign tables, and graph visualizations that enhance understanding of each analytical stage.
